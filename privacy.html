Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Welcome to Split & Snap ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our web and mobile application.

🔒 Summary: Split & Snap does NOT collect, transmit, store on remote servers, or share any of your personal data with third parties. All calculation data and preferences remain strictly on your local device.

1. Data Collection & Storage

Split & Snap operates 100% locally on your device.

  • We do not create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • Any bills, receipt details, expense splits, payment notes (e.g., IBAN/Revolut tags), or local history logs entered into the app are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage).
  • We do not operate backend servers or databases to collect your data.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because we do not collect any data, we do not sell, rent, or share any personal information or usage data with any third parties.

When you use the "Share Summary" feature, the app passes text formatted by you directly to your device's native sharing menu (e.g., WhatsApp, Viber, SMS). We have no access to who you share this information with.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Split & Snap does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us via our official repository or developer profile.
